

A principle engineer oversees the design of PICOS’s architecture and protocols. The job title of potential candidates could be System Architect or Engineering Architect. The job includes:

Develop Engineering Design Document for all PICOS features and enhancement. The Engineering Design Document must include use cases, workflow, deployment environment, dependency of software modules, risk analysis and mitigation, control plane and data plane implementation, and management interfaces. 

Interface with PLM and Solution Architect to define use cases and workflows.

Interface with the Engineering team to define architecture design, management scheme, risk analysis, and implementation assumptions.

Develop prototypes to validate the ideas and assumptions.

Interface with Pre-sales on training of new features and interface with post-sales on tools to analyze field tickets.

Interface with platform vendors and ASIC vendors on the integration with SDK and drivers.

Experience with L2/L3 protocols

ASIC programming experience
