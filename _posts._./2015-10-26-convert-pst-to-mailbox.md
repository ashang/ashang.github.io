


Share my means here, worked from Ubuntu Linux.
Before this success, lots of searched results always lead to outdated solutions, for old versions.
* First, get the `.pst` files ready
* Install package `pst-utils`, which contains utils to convert contacts and mails.
    * I used `readpst` with `-e` option to convert `.pst` to MH with extentions.
    * `man readpst` or `readpst -h` for more options
* At `thunderbird` side, I used this addon to import the converted result mail directory.
    * Author’s link: ImportExportTools (https://freeshell.de/~kaosmos/mboximport-en.html)
    * Author’s link: ImportExportTools (https://freeshell.de/~kaosmos/importexporttools-en.html)
    * Mozilla addons page: ImportExportTools (https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/thunderbird/addon/importexporttools/)
Good luck.
