We're in the same situation: Looking for a decent, all-in-one BT headset that doesn't come with the outdated HFP 1.5 profile. You would think it shouldn't be this hard to find one years after the standard has been passed; then again, if you're a Windows user, to this day only the Bluesoleil stack supports HFP 1.6 and the (unfortunately) optional wideband codec. I guess we're a small minority that Microsoft and other big companies can easily afford to simply ignore.

Testing with my Jabra Extreme 2, a 2009 MBP didnt seem to support the newer HFP version either, but this may be due to the rather outdated BT module. iOS on the other hand works just fine. The wideband-effect is nowhere near as nice as the in-the-room feeling you get when using a PC or notebook with a usable pair of speakers, but definitely much better than being limited to narrowband.

I've tried many headsets and earpieces over the years and fondly remember a pair of Nokia BH-604s which offered decent music quality, great range and superb battery life (15 – 20H). Even if they had been wideband (which at the time wasn't an option), calls would probably have had a muffled quality to them for lack of a mic-boom. Telephony is nearly always an afterthought, even in $200 cans, and the Logitech H800 is probably closest to what we are looking for.

Here's to hoping that their replacement model comes with proper wideband-audio support. I'd gladly pay them or any other company $200 and more for it if they decided to cater to a smaller market and go premium. Heck, maybe even MS wakes up from their long slumber one day and adds support for HFP 1.6 ... one can dream, right? :P
