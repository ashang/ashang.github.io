- Unrestricted access to the complete Google Play Store and its selection of over 1.5 million apps.
- Popular media player app KODI™ (XBMC) comes pre-installed on all our MINIX Media Hubs for Android™.

- Built-in VPN (Virtual Proxy Network) capabilities.
- Incredibly low power consumption that’s both eco-friendly and cost-saving.
